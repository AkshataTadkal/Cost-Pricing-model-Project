# 💎 Pricing Intelligence Platform

> An AI-powered Manufacturing Pricing & Decision Intelligence Platform built with **Snowflake**, **Snowpark**, **Streamlit**, and **Snowflake Cortex AI**.

---

# 📌 Overview

The **Pricing Intelligence Platform** is an enterprise-grade application designed to help manufacturing organizations make intelligent, data-driven pricing decisions. It combines deterministic pricing calculations with AI-powered business reasoning to optimize pricing strategies, maximize profitability, and support executive decision-making.

The platform integrates manufacturing costs, customer agreements, plant capacity, competitor pricing, and market intelligence into a unified dashboard. Using **Snowflake Cortex AI**, it generates explainable recommendations, executive summaries, risk assessments, and pricing insights while ensuring all financial calculations remain accurate and auditable.

Unlike traditional spreadsheet-based pricing systems, this platform provides real-time analytics, interactive simulations, and AI-assisted decision support within a secure Snowflake-native environment.

# 📌 Conclusion

The **Pricing Intelligence Platform** transforms traditional manufacturing pricing into an intelligent, AI-assisted decision-making system.
